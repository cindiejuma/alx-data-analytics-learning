# Learning Reflection: Introduction to Data Analytics with Spreadsheets

As part of my data analytics learning journey with ALX, I completed the short course **Introduction to Data Analytics with Spreadsheets**. This course provided an opportunity to refresh my statistical knowledge, reflect on my existing skills, and explore new ways of approaching data analysis.

## 1. What I Worked On

Throughout the course, I revisited key concepts in spreadsheet-based data analytics, including:

- Data preparation, cleaning, and integrity.
- Descriptive analysis and data visualization.
- Spreadsheet functions and statistical distributions.
- Identifying patterns and interpreting relationships between variables.
- Lines of best fit, correlation, and R-squared.
- Model accuracy, residuals, MAE, MSE, and RMSE.
- Hypothesis testing, statistical significance, and parametric and non-parametric tests.
- Using AI tools to support spreadsheet workflows and data analysis.

## 2. What I Achieved

Having studied statistics previously, I was already familiar with many of these concepts. However, this course helped refresh my knowledge and gave me an opportunity to reflect on how I apply statistical principles in practical data analysis.

One of my key takeaways was learning how tools like **Gemini can support data analysis**. This expanded my understanding of how AI can complement existing analytical skills and improve the way I approach data-related tasks.

## 3. Challenges and Lessons Learned

Balancing the course with work and other responsibilities was challenging. I underestimated the time I would need to complete it, which taught me the importance of setting realistic learning goals and managing my time more effectively.

I also found hypothesis testing challenging to fully understand using the available learning resources. Rather than treating completion of the course as mastery of every topic, I recognize the need to revisit this area and explore additional resources to strengthen my understanding.

## 4. A Moment Worth Remembering

My biggest achievement was simply reaching the end of the course despite the competing demands on my time.

This experience reminded me that learning does not always mean discovering something entirely new. Sometimes, it means revisiting what you already know, strengthening your understanding, identifying gaps, and being willing to keep learning.

## Key Takeaway

**Continuous learning is about more than completing a course; it is about recognizing what you know, identifying what you need to improve, and taking the next step.**

I look forward to applying these lessons in my data analytics work and continuing to develop my technical skills.
